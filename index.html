Frequently asked questions

What can I expect from Ghost Stories?

An intimate evening of true ghost stories, mind reading and psychological illusion, held in a hidden chamber inside 1923 Prohibition Bar. It is mysterious and unsettling rather than gory, and most guests laugh as often as they gasp.

What time should I arrive for the show?

Please arrive about 15 minutes before showtime to check in, find your seat and order a drink before the lights go down.

Is Ghost Stories suitable for all ages?

Ghost Stories is for guests 21 and over. Please bring a valid photo ID, as the bar checks at the door.

Do I need to purchase tickets in advance?

Yes, we recommend it. The audience is kept small, so shows often sell out. Tap Get Tickets to choose your date.

How long is the performance?

About 100 minutes, with an intermission.

Where is the Ghost Stories show performed?

In a secret hidden chamber inside 1923 Prohibition Bar at Mandalay Bay Resort & Casino, 3930 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas.

Can I enjoy drinks during the show?

Yes. 1923 Prohibition Bar serves its craft cocktails and drinks before and during the show.

Is the show interactive?

Yes. Kent invites guests to take part in mind reading and the séance. Volunteers are always invited, never forced, and you can simply watch if you prefer.
