The Guest in Seat Eleven

On a slow Thursday a woman bought one ticket for seat eleven. She arrived early, ordered nothing, and watched the whole show without blinking.

When the lights came up the seat was empty, and the bartender swore no one had left through the door. On the table sat the ticket stub, dated 1923.
